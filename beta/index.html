PWS Console Beta

PWS Console is currently being tested before broader release across iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Local Weather, optional Personal Weather Station integration, radar, widgets, and Apple Watch complications.

What to Expect

Beta software may contain bugs or incomplete behavior, and features or presentation may change as testing continues. Please install available updates through TestFlight so your feedback reflects reasonably current behavior.

What We'd Like Tested

Use PWS Console as you normally would and let us know where the experience is unclear or unreliable. In particular, please exercise:

  • Local Weather and device-location behavior.
  • Radar viewing and refresh.
  • Optional connection to your own compatible Weather Underground Personal Weather Station.
  • Refresh behavior throughout the app.
  • Station and Local Weather on Apple Watch.
  • Home-screen widgets and Apple Watch complications.
  • Recovery after weather information or connectivity is temporarily unavailable.

For troubleshooting and additional help, visit Support.

Send Feedback

Send beta feedback to support@pwsconsole.com. When practical, include:

  • The affected device: iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, widget, or complication.
  • The iOS, iPadOS, or watchOS version.
  • The PWS Console version and build number.
  • The affected feature.
  • What you expected and what actually happened.
  • A screenshot when it helps explain the problem.

Never send your Weather Underground API key, passwords, or other credentials in email or screenshots.
