Privacy Policy

PWS Console for Android

PWS Console is designed as a weather application, not a data-collection platform. This policy explains what information the Android app uses, why it uses it, and where that information goes.

Overview

PWS Console for Android provides weather information without requiring a PWS Console account. The current application does not contain advertising, analytics, or a crash-reporting service, and PWS Console does not operate a weather-data backend.

PWS Console does not sell personal information or use information for cross-app or cross-website advertising tracking.

Saved Home and Location

PWS Console for Android currently uses manually configured Saved Home locations rather than your device's current location. You may enter a Saved Home using a place, postal code, region, country, and selected time zone. The application package declares Android precise-location, approximate-location, and Wi-Fi-state permissions contributed by its mapping library. PWS Console v1.0 does not request runtime location access or use your device's location. Optional device-location support may be offered in a future version only with your permission.

When you choose to verify a Saved Home, the place, postal code, region, country, and selected time zone information you enter is sent to Android's system geocoder to find possible geographic matches. PWS Console verifies a single matching result and asks you to refine the entry when the result is ambiguous; it does not silently choose among multiple matches.

Verified precise Saved Home coordinates and applicable location settings are stored locally on your device. When you request weather, radar, or map features, the app sends those precise coordinates and applicable location settings directly to the third-party services needed to supply those features.

Personal Weather Stations

Connecting your own Weather Underground Personal Weather Station is optional. If you choose to configure one, you provide a Weather Underground Station ID and your own Weather Underground API key. The app sends the Station ID and API key directly to Weather Underground over HTTPS to request observations.

Your API key is encrypted using Android Keystore protection. The encrypted credential preferences are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so the key is not expected to restore to a new device or fresh installation and you may need to enter it again. On an ordinary same-device signed app update, the key is intended and expected to remain available, but that behavior is not guaranteed until it has been physically validated.

PWS Console does not operate a backend that receives or stores your Weather Underground credentials.

Weather, Radar, and Map Providers

PWS Console communicates directly with third-party services to provide geocoding, observations, forecasts, radar, and map functionality. Depending on the feature and your Saved Home, these services may include Android's system geocoder, Weather Underground, NOAA / National Weather Service, Open-Meteo, RainViewer, U.S. Geological Survey / The National Map, and map resources used by MapLibre.

These services may receive information needed to fulfill a request, such as a manually entered location query, precise Saved Home coordinates, selected time zone, Weather Underground Station ID, or—if you configure it—your Weather Underground API key. They operate under their own terms and privacy practices.

Information Stored on Your Device

PWS Console stores application settings, Saved Home configuration, verified coordinates, and optional Weather Underground station information locally on your device. Weather and forecast information used by PWS Console is held in bounded in-memory application caches to support the app while it is running.

Retention, Removal, and Permissions

You can update or remove your Saved Home and Weather Underground configuration in the app. Saving a blank Weather Underground API key removes its locally stored encrypted credential value. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling PWS Console removes app-managed local data, subject to normal Android device behavior. Ordinary settings are eligible for normal Android backup, subject to your device, account, encryption, and Android platform behavior; the encrypted Weather Underground credential preferences are excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

PWS Console uses Internet and network-state permissions. Its mapping library contributes declared Android precise-location, approximate-location, and Wi-Fi-state permissions, but PWS Console v1.0 does not request runtime device-location access or use device location. It does not request background-location, notification, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage permissions.

Tracking and Advertising

PWS Console for Android does not contain advertising and does not use your information for cross-app advertising tracking.

Accounts

PWS Console does not require a PWS Console account or sign-in. A separate Weather Underground account may be necessary if you choose to obtain and use your own Weather Underground API key.

Public Website

The public website at pwsconsole.com is separate from the Android application. It does not receive Saved Home configuration or Weather Underground credentials from the app. The website is served and protected through Cloudflare, which may process standard network information needed to deliver and protect the site under its own policies.

Children's Privacy

PWS Console is a general-purpose weather application and is not specifically directed to children. PWS Console does not knowingly operate a service intended to collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as PWS Console for Android evolves. Material changes affecting how information is handled will be reflected on this page.
