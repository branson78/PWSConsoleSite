Privacy Policy

PWS Console for Fire TV

Effective Date: September 14, 2026

PWS Console respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the PWS Console for Fire TV application handles information when you use the application.

PWS Console for Fire TV provides location-based weather conditions, radar, forecasts, and weather alerts without requiring a PWS Console account or sign-in.

Information You Provide

Home ZIP Code

PWS Console for Fire TV asks you to provide a Home ZIP Code so the application can determine the geographic area for which weather information should be displayed.

Your Home ZIP Code is stored locally on your Fire TV device and is used to determine an approximate geographic location for weather services. The ZIP Code is transmitted to a third-party geographic lookup service as necessary to resolve it to a geographic location.

PWS Console does not require your name, email address, telephone number, or other account information to use the Fire TV application.

Location Information

PWS Console for Fire TV does not request or use your Fire TV device's GPS or location permission.

Instead, the application determines the location for weather information from the Home ZIP Code you provide. The resulting approximate geographic coordinates are used when requesting weather, radar, forecast, and alert information from external services.

Third-Party Services

The application communicates directly with third-party Internet services to provide its weather, location, radar, and mapping functionality.

These services include:

Zippopotam.us

Used to resolve the Home ZIP Code you provide to an approximate geographic location.

NOAA / National Weather Service (NWS)

Used to obtain weather observations, forecasts, weather alerts, radar information, and related weather data.

Esri

Used to provide geographic map services displayed with weather radar.

When the application communicates with these services, the service may receive information necessary to process the request, such as a ZIP Code or approximate geographic coordinates, as well as standard Internet connection information such as the device's public IP address.

These third-party services operate independently of PWS Console and may process information according to their own privacy practices.

Additional information about applicable privacy practices is available from:

  • National Weather Service Privacy Policy
  • Esri Privacy Statement

Information Stored on Your Fire TV

The application stores a limited amount of information locally on your Fire TV device, including your Home ZIP Code, application settings, resolved location information, and weather-related data used to operate the application and maintain functionality when external services are temporarily unavailable.

This locally stored information is used to provide application functionality and is not used to create a PWS Console user profile.

You can remove information stored locally by the application by clearing the application's data through Fire TV settings or by uninstalling PWS Console for Fire TV.

PWS Console Accounts

PWS Console for Fire TV does not require or provide a PWS Console user account or sign-in.

The application does not collect usernames or passwords for a PWS Console service.

Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

PWS Console for Fire TV does not contain advertising.

PWS Console does not use the Fire TV application to track you across other applications or websites for advertising purposes.

The application does not include third-party advertising or behavioral analytics services for monitoring how you use the application.

Sale or Sharing of Personal Information

PWS Console does not sell your personal information.

PWS Console does not share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Information necessary to provide weather, mapping, radar, and geographic functionality is transmitted directly by the application to the third-party services described in this Privacy Policy.

Device Permissions and Sensitive Information

PWS Console for Fire TV does not require access to your:

  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Precise device location
  • Payment or financial information

Data Retention and Deletion

Information maintained by PWS Console for Fire TV is primarily stored locally on your Fire TV device.

You can remove this locally stored information by clearing the application's data through Fire TV settings or by uninstalling the application.

Information transmitted to third-party weather, mapping, and geographic services may be subject to those services' independent retention and privacy practices.

Because PWS Console for Fire TV does not require a PWS Console account, there is no PWS Console cloud account or associated account profile that must be deleted.

Children's Privacy

PWS Console for Fire TV is a general-purpose weather application and is not directed specifically toward children.

PWS Console does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Fire TV application.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as PWS Console for Fire TV evolves or as its services and privacy practices change.

When this policy is updated, the revised version will be published on the PWS Console website and the effective date at the top of the policy will be updated.
