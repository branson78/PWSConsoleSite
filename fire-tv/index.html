Privacy & support

A public weather experience with a clear boundary.

Fire TV uses the Home ZIP you choose for weather requests. It does not expose personal-station credentials, private PWS Console runtime information, or administrative controls. For help, visit Support; see the Fire TV Privacy Policy for how PWS Console handles information.
